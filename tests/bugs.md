# Bugs

Defects that a test run has confirmed, one row each. A row is added when a failure is confirmed as a defect, with its severity, and updated in the commit that fixes it. The case named is the one that fails without the fix.

Evidence for an open bug is kept on the tester's machine under `tests/runs/kept/<ID>/`; it is not committed.

## Severity

Whoever opens a row gives it a severity (Rich, 2026-11-17). The definitions are his:

| Severity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Critical (1) | Prevents the completion of a primary flow, with no reasonable workaround. Also significant data loss, a security vulnerability, or the app being unavailable |
| High (2) | Significantly impacts a primary flow but a workaround exists, or completely prevents the completion of a secondary flow. Materially impacts the user experience |
| Medium (3) | Impacts functionality within a primary or secondary flow but does not prevent the user from completing it. A reasonable workaround exists, or the impact is limited to specific scenarios or conditions |
| Low (4) | Minimal impact on functionality or on completing a flow: cosmetic issues, minor inconsistencies, formatting errors, wording |

Which flows are primary (Claude's reading, confirmed by Rich, 2026-11-17): the **primary flows** are the journeys in `tests/catalog/journeys.md` (finishing a card on the phone: open a photo, straighten it, crop, correct the color, add a logo, then share or download it, from a Safari tab or the home screen icon). Everything else is a **secondary flow**: front and back pairs, corner shots, edits files, resuming a session, the wide-screen layout and its keyboard, developer links.

## Decision

No bug is fixed until Rich has reviewed it (Rich, 2026-11-17). After a run or a phone checklist, all of its new rows are put to him, most severe first, and he says for each: **Fix** (before the release) or **Open for now** (it may ship). An accepted bug that will be fixed later is Open for now, and its Decision may say when ("to be fixed with the detection design"): it never holds up a release (Rich, 2026-11-24). A row not yet reviewed reads **Not reviewed**. The `test` skill has the steps.
